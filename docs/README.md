## An initiative to sustain a bright human-based future

![image](./assets/intiative.jpg)

Polysance facilitates education and knowledge transfer in the area of electronic computing, including programming and integrated circuit design, using the methods and approach to problem definition, analysis and solution demonstrated by Charles H. Moore. These methods include the programming methodology named Forth, and the silicon design tools and computers produced by Mr. Moore and his colleagues between the 1980s and the present time. The approach is heavily cross-disciplinary and is driven by first principles.

Polysance permeates deep focus on _machine intelligence_, the cybernetic concept singular and distinct from McCarthy's "artificial intelligence". History, context, and the vital necessary skills are part of the curriculum such that students learn a wholly novel and sophisticated technique that not simply compliments, but suggests an alternative model to intelligent machinery.
